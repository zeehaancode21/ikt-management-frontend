Need Help?

Why do I need to configure this?

RFI emails are sent from your Gmail account. We need your authorization to send emails on your behalf.

Is my token secure?

Yes! Your token is encrypted and stored securely. We never share it with third parties.

What if my token expires?

If you see "Token Invalid", simply update it with a new token from your Google Cloud Console.

Can I use a non-Gmail email?

No, this feature currently requires a Gmail account with OAuth enabled.

What permissions do I need to grant?

Grant access to "Gmail API" - specifically the ability to send emails.
